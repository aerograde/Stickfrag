          A stickman FPS you can play anywhere

          StickFrag is a free online multiplayer stickman shooter. You see the fight through your stickman's eyes, but the whole world is drawn like a printed comic: fat ink lines, flat colours and halftone shadows. You join a match that is already running, and bots fill it when few people are online.

          Team deathmatch and gun game for up to 14 players. No download, no account, and it works on a school Chromebook.

          Team deathmatch

          Blue against red, eight against eight. Pick a main gun, a sidearm and a grenade for every life: any of the twelve guns, from the SMG to the RPG. The sniper, the machine gun and the hand cannon wait between the two halves of the map.

          Gun game

          Every two kills give you the next gun: M4, AK, machine gun, SMG, shotgun, sniper, hand cannon, revolver, pistol and the silenced pistol. Then the knife: one knife kill wins.

          Five maps

          A fenced yard, a main street, a farm, a huge rail depot and a sea port. Up to 14 players fight in each match, 20 on the depot. See the maps

          Questions from brands

          How do you count a view?

          A view is one sighting: your art is on screen, big enough to read, not behind a wall, for at least one second. The game counts views and our server checks and caps the numbers. Views come from players' browsers, so our reports call them estimates. Player numbers come from our servers.

          Where do the forecast numbers come from?

          From our daily players, the average play time and how many players come back. You can see the exact numbers we use below the calculator.

          What if fewer players come than you forecast?

          We add free days to your campaign until it reaches the views we forecast when you booked. You never pay more than the price you agreed.

          Do you track players or use cookies?

          No. We set no ad cookies and build no profiles, and we do not target anybody. Every player in a match sees the same brand. That also keeps your campaign clear of the EU ban on profiling-based ads to minors.

          Who plays StickFrag?

          Players on desktop browsers, mostly in Europe and North America. Many are teenagers and young adults. They play in short sessions, often with friends.

          Can I change my art during a campaign?

          Yes, once a week, free. Send the new art by Thursday and it goes live the next Monday.

          Is there a minimum?

          One slot for one week. No contract after that, and no automatic renewal.

          Will players see an “AD” mark?

          Yes. Paid art carries a small “AD” mark, and the game menu lists our sponsors. The law requires that players can tell an ad from the game.
